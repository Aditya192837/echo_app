Plain-Language Note on Overfitting

Overfitting is clearly visible in the training curves: by epoch 50, training AUC reaches approximately 0.996 while validation AUC plateaus around 0.910. Concurrently, validation loss begins rising after mid-training. To prevent degraded generalization, model selection selected the best validation checkpoint at Epoch 26 (0-indexed epoch 25, val AUC 0.921) rather than the final epoch.

About the Project

Student research demo exploring deep learning for echocardiography.

Dataset

The model was trained and evaluated on the EchoNet-Dynamic dataset from Stanford University, comprising 10,030 apical 4-chamber (A4C) echocardiogram videos (112×112 resolution) paired with expert cardiac measurements.

The training target is binary: Ejection Fraction (EF) < 50% defines abnormal (reduced) left ventricular function (labeled 1), while EF ≥ 50% is normal (labeled 0). The dataset is split into 7,465 training, 1,288 validation, and 1,277 test videos (~22% overall abnormal prevalence).

How the Model Works

The model architecture is a 3D convolutional video network based on X3D-M with a cross-view fusion head (from the OmniRead-Echo framework). In this configuration, it operates as a single-view model on the Apical 4-Chamber view.

During inference, the model samples 16 frames from the video (every 4th frame, spanning ~64 frames or ~1.3 seconds at 50 fps, capturing approximately one full cardiac cycle). Each frame is resized to 112×112. The model outputs a single probability estimating the likelihood of reduced LV function.

Evaluation & Scope

The model was evaluated on a held-out test subset of 500 videos (115 abnormal, 23% abnormal rate), achieving a Test AUC of 0.867 (90% confidence interval: 0.836 to 0.896) and a Best Validation AUC of 0.921.

The decision threshold (0.0974) was selected on the validation set to balance sensitivity (83.5%) and specificity (71.7%), producing a Negative Predictive Value of 93.6% and Positive Predictive Value of 46.8%.

Limitations & Constraints

  • Single-view limitation: Clinical echocardiography examines multiple views (A4C, A2C, PLAX, PSAX) for comprehensive assessment. This demo uses only A4C.
  • Single-hospital cohort: Trained on scans acquired from a single healthcare system; accuracy may vary on other ultrasound machines, transducers, or patient cohorts.
  • Uncalibrated probabilities: Model output reflects raw sigmoid activation; numbers represent relative ranking rather than calibrated epidemiological risks.
